Consumer Psychology · Packaging & Advertising

What catches our eye, and what do we take away from it? Drawing on my background in human factors psychology, I analyzed Diptyque’s Philosykos packaging and a lululemon campaign poster to explore how design choices guide attention, affect readability, and shape impressions of a brand. In both projects, I profiled the intended audience, evaluated the design from their perspective, and used that analysis to propose improvements.
